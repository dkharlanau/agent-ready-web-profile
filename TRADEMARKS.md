## Status and scope

No registered-trademark status, registry clearance, exclusive right to the acronym, or absence of conflicting rights is asserted by this repository. A project-name policy is not a trademark registration or legal opinion. Do not display the registered symbol for this project without verified registration and applicable scope.

The software license remains Apache-2.0. Its trademark provision is separate from its software permissions. The designated media/research reuse terms remain in `docs/media/rights.json`; this policy does not expand those terms to third-party logos or material and does not narrow permissions already granted by a license or applicable law.

## Accurate references

Use the project name to identify the actual project, source or compatible implementation. Link to the canonical project when attribution is appropriate. Describe a modified distribution as a fork or adaptation and distinguish its maintainer. Please choose a distinct brand for an independent product rather than implying that it is the official ARWP distribution.

Do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification or approval by ARWP or by an upstream provider. Compatibility with a protocol is not an affiliation. Third-party names in documentation identify their respective products or standards; this repository does not grant rights in those marks.

## Review before a commercial brand commitment

Record the proposed name and variants, target jurisdictions, goods/services, relevant classes, search date, registries searched, broader use searches, potentially similar marks and reviewer conclusion. Review resemblance in sound and meaning as well as exact spelling. WIPO's database and a domain-name search alone are not comprehensive clearance. Escalate uncertain conflicts to a qualified trademark professional for the relevant market.

No such clearance report is claimed to have been completed here. Product identity, software licensing and brand clearance remain separate facts.
