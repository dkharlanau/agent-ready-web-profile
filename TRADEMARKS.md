Policy date: 2026-09-09. Maintainer: Dzmitryi Kharlanau.

## Project-reserved naming roles

“Reserved” in this repository means that the project assigns a name a specific current role. It does **not** mean government registration, legal exclusivity or completed trademark clearance.

Current project roles are published in `docs/project/profile.json` and summarized on the public [Names & Marks](https://dkharlanau.github.io/agent-ready-web-profile/project/marks.html) page. They include:

- **Goose ARWP** — canonical product-facing name;
- **Goose** — short product name;
- **Agent-Ready Web Profile** — technical project/foundation name;
- **ARWP** — established technical abbreviation; no exclusive acronym rights are claimed;
- **Get Found.** — primary tagline;
- **Goose Radar / Map / Plan / Patch / Proof / Watch** — namespaced product modules;
- **BraidGraph** — project-defined technical primitive;
- **Cite Goose** — deprecated legacy public name retained only where history/path compatibility requires it;
- **SignalBraid** — historical design direction, not the current public brand.

## Status and scope

No registered-trademark status, registry clearance, exclusive right to the acronym, or absence of conflicting rights is asserted by this repository. A project-name policy is not a trademark registration or legal opinion. Do not display the registered symbol for this project without verified registration and applicable scope.

The software license remains PolyForm Strict 1.0.0. Its trademark provision is separate from its software permissions. The designated media/research reuse terms remain in `docs/media/rights.json`; this policy does not expand those terms to third-party logos or material and does not narrow permissions already granted by a license or applicable law.

## Accurate references

Use the project name to identify the actual project, source or compatible implementation. Link to the canonical project when attribution is appropriate. Describe a modified distribution as a fork or adaptation and distinguish its maintainer. Please choose a distinct brand for an independent product rather than implying that it is the official Goose / ARWP distribution.

Do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification or approval by Goose, ARWP, MetalHatsCats or an upstream provider. Compatibility with a protocol, contribution to the repository or a collaboration discussion is not an affiliation. Third-party names in documentation identify their respective products or standards; this repository does not grant rights in those marks.

## Review before a commercial brand commitment

Record the proposed name and variants, target jurisdictions, goods/services, relevant classes, search date, registries searched, broader use searches, potentially similar marks and reviewer conclusion. Review resemblance in sound and meaning as well as exact spelling. WIPO's database and a domain-name search alone are not comprehensive clearance. Escalate uncertain conflicts to a qualified trademark professional for the relevant market.

No such clearance report is claimed to have been completed here. Product identity, software licensing, project-reserved naming roles and brand clearance remain separate facts.
