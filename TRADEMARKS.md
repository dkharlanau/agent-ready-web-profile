# Project name, product brand and third-party marks

Policy date: 2026-09-07. Maintainer: Dzmitryi Kharlanau.

The technical open-source project name is **Agent-Ready Web Profile**, abbreviated **ARWP**. Canonical project: https://dkharlanau.github.io/agent-ready-web-profile/ ; source: https://github.com/dkharlanau/agent-ready-web-profile .

The product-facing brand introduced on 2026-09-07 is **SiteCurrent**. The intended relationship is:

> **SiteCurrent — adaptive website improvement, powered by ARWP.**

SiteCurrent does not rename the repository, npm package, ARWP profile/compatibility terminology or historical technical records. It is the human-facing product identity for the adaptive website-improvement experience built on the ARWP technical foundation.

## Status and scope

No registered-trademark status, registry clearance, exclusive right to **SiteCurrent**, **Agent-Ready Web Profile**, **ARWP**, their visual marks or absence of conflicting rights is asserted by this repository. A project-name policy is not a trademark registration or legal opinion. Do not display the registered symbol for these names without verified registration and applicable scope.

A general web search for the proposed SiteCurrent name was performed on 2026-09-07 as a preliminary naming check. This was not a comprehensive trademark clearance search and did not establish domain availability or rights in any jurisdiction or goods/services class.

The software license remains Apache-2.0. Its trademark provision is separate from its software permissions. The designated media/research reuse terms remain in `docs/media/rights.json`; this policy does not expand those terms to third-party logos or material and does not narrow permissions already granted by a license or applicable law.

## Accurate references

Use **SiteCurrent** for the product-facing system when discussing the adaptive improvement workflow, value proposition or branded product experience.

Use **Agent-Ready Web Profile / ARWP** when referring to the open-source project, package, source repository, profile contracts, resolver/interoperability work or technical compatibility.

For attribution where both are useful, prefer:

> SiteCurrent / Agent-Ready Web Profile (ARWP)

Describe a modified distribution as a fork or adaptation and distinguish its maintainer. Please choose a distinct brand for an independent product rather than implying that it is the official SiteCurrent/ARWP distribution.

Do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification or approval by SiteCurrent/ARWP or by an upstream provider. Compatibility with a protocol is not an affiliation. Third-party names in documentation identify their respective products or standards; this repository does not grant rights in those marks.

## Review before a commercial brand commitment

Before a commercial launch, record the proposed name and variants, target jurisdictions, goods/services, relevant classes, search date, registries searched, broader use searches, potentially similar marks and reviewer conclusion. Review resemblance in sound and meaning as well as exact spelling. WIPO's database and a domain-name search alone are not comprehensive clearance. Escalate uncertain conflicts to a qualified trademark professional for the relevant market.

No such comprehensive clearance report is claimed to have been completed here. Product identity, software licensing and brand clearance remain separate facts.
