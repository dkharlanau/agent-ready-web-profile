001

Public repository discovery and interest

Use repository topics that match the actual job

A public repository may be hard to find under the terms its users recognize.

v1.0.0inferredPattern reviewed 18 Oct 2026

What to do

  • Choose a small set of accurate domain, capability and platform topics from the implemented project.
  • Review the repository description and README entry against the same user task.

Check the change

  • Each chosen topic has a source-visible capability; planned features and unrelated trending topics are excluded.

What result to look for

Observe dated GitHub search-panel presence separately from views and use.

Why it may help: An accurate repository entry may help relevant users evaluate the project; views, clones and CI alone do not establish use.

Watch out for: Topic stuffing or presenting topic changes as Google ranking gains.

003

Public repository discovery and interest

Archive daily repository traffic without double counting

Rolling traffic exports overlap and cannot be added as independent weeks.

v1.0.0inferredPattern reviewed 18 Oct 2026

What to do

  • Capture day-grain traffic within the provider retention window and preserve actual capture time.
  • Join repository ID, metric and UTC date; retain revisions and missing dates. Never add daily unique counts into unique people.

Check the change

  • Two overlapping synthetic captures do not double the shared days; incomplete weeks have no full-week delta.

What result to look for

Compare two complete equal UTC windows using counts, not summed uniques.

Why it may help: An accurate repository entry may help relevant users evaluate the project; views, clones and CI alone do not establish use.

Watch out for: Adding rolling totals or relabelling unique cloners as customers.

004

Public repository discovery and interest

Show CI activity beside clone traffic

Own automation can coincide with a clone spike without revealing who cloned.

v1.0.0inferredPattern reviewed 18 Oct 2026

What to do

  • Retrieve workflow-run counts for the same UTC interval and retain provider receipts.
  • Show runs and clones as different units; label external interest unconfirmed unless independent usage evidence exists. Never subtract runs to invent external clones.

Check the change

  • A high-clone/high-CI fixture stays unattributed; failed CI collection is missing, not zero runs.

What result to look for

Compare dated workflow runs, clone counts and independently observed first-use outcomes.

Why it may help: An accurate repository entry may help relevant users evaluate the project; views, clones and CI alone do not establish use.

Watch out for: Interpreting a clone residual or correlation as external adoption.
