Boundaries

Entity consistency is not authority by itself.

The graph makes project identity and relationships explicit. It does not create independent adoption, prove a claim, grant access, establish authorship for third-party material, or guarantee search/AI inclusion. Claim truth still comes from the evidence attached to each claim or research surface.
