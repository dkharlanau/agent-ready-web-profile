Trust should be inspectable.

Goose ARWP does not use a single trust or readiness score. This page exposes who maintains the project, what rights and naming boundaries apply, how collaboration and governance work, what evidence it uses, how corrections are handled, how releases can be verified, what remains external or planned, and what none of those signals prove.

Security

The resolver accepts public URLs and parses untrusted web metadata, so network boundaries and read-only behavior are security-relevant. Reports should follow the repository security policy rather than public exploit disclosure.

Corrections

Material published factual errors are recorded in a public ledger. Benchmark improvements are preserved as revisions rather than relabeled as corrections, and superseded claims remain discoverable.

Build provenance & SBOM

The npm workflow uses GitHub OIDC/trusted publishing. The next npm publish is configured to pack the exact tarball, generate a CycloneDX dependency SBOM, attest provenance and the SBOM to that same tarball, then publish that exact artifact.

gh attestation verify <tarball> -R dkharlanau/agent-ready-web-profile

The MCP Registry publisher dependency is also version- and SHA-256-pinned before execution.

Boundary: provenance and SBOM attestations prove scoped build/dependency facts; they do not prove that the artifact is vulnerability-free or correct.

Reuse rights

Software remains Apache-2.0. Designated Goose-authored press/research/media material is available under CC BY 4.0, including explicit AI/ML reuse permission where covered by the rights manifest. Other content and third-party material keep their own rights boundary.

What this Trust Center does not prove

It does not prove that Goose ARWP is legally compliant in every jurisdiction, vulnerability-free, universally adopted, endorsed by upstream protocol owners, guaranteed to rank in search/AI systems, or correct outside each claim's stated evidence and date. Trust signals are useful because they are inspectable and bounded, not because they remove judgment.
