Public corrections policy

Corrections stay visible.

When ARWP confirms a material factual error that was already published, the project records what was wrong, the corrected statement, affected canonical URLs and evidence for the change. Historical records and superseded claims are not silently rewritten.

Current ledger

No material published factual correction is recorded as of 5 September 2026.

This does not mean the project has never changed. Engineering improvements, benchmark revisions and protocol-status updates are recorded in their own dated histories. For example, the 2026-08-28 resolver benchmark improvement from 81/100 to 86/100 followed a general eligibility-policy change on an unchanged reviewed corpus; the historical 81/100 result remains valid for the earlier implementation and is not erased.

What becomes a correction

  • a published factual statement is shown to be wrong;
  • a version/date/source attribution was materially incorrect;
  • a canonical claim overstated what its evidence supported;
  • a public page incorrectly represented a capability as active, verified or independently adopted.

Normal product evolution, new measurements, changed upstream specifications and improved resolver policy are revisions, not corrections, unless an earlier published statement was itself false.
