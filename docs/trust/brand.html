Registration and clearance

No registered-trademark status, completed clearance search, exclusive acronym rights or absence of conflicting rights is claimed. This page is a project-name policy, not a registry record or legal opinion. Do not attach a registered symbol without verified registration and applicable scope.

A meaningful clearance review records the proposed name and variants, jurisdictions, goods/services, relevant classes, search date, registries and broader uses, similar marks and a reviewer conclusion. Exact-name, domain and WIPO searches alone cannot settle every conflict.
