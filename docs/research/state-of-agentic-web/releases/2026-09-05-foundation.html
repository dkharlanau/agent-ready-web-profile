Interpretation

  • Broader discovery coverage did not yet outperform the strongest simpler strategy on the frozen reviewed corpus.
  • The general OpenAPI search-eligibility correction improved resolver-union from 81/100 to 86/100 without changing reviewed ground truth.
  • Resolver regret remains visible: six decisions were cases where a simpler strategy was correct while resolver-union was wrong.
  • Resolver-union had zero uniquely-correct decisions on this frozen corpus at the evidence date, so discovery breadth alone is not treated as product progress.

Limitations

  • This is a reviewed engineering sample, not a representative survey of the public web.
  • The cohort is documentation-heavy and was selected for Resolver decision-quality evaluation, not adoption measurement.
  • The release does not establish ARWP adoption, search ranking gains, AI citation gains, answer quality, token savings or production interoperability beyond the recorded tests.
  • Owner-controlled ARWP reference sites are excluded from the independent aggregate.
  • Live publisher capabilities can drift after the frozen evidence date; historical ground truth is not silently rewritten.
