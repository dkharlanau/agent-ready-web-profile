Open Media & AI Reuse Pack

No permission request required

Use ARWP material. Credit the source.

Designated ARWP-authored press material, research visuals and media assets are available under CC BY 4.0. Journalists, publishers, researchers, creators and AI/ML workflows may reuse covered material without asking for separate permission, subject to attribution and the stated scope.

Source: Agent-Ready Web Profile (ARWP) — https://dkharlanau.github.io/agent-ready-web-profile/ · CC BY 4.0

Press & news

You may quote, reproduce, crop, resize, translate and adapt covered ARWP material for editorial, news, commercial publication and social distribution.

No email approval is required. Link back to the canonical ARWP project or the specific source page when practical.

AI & machine use

Covered material may be used for AI search indexing, training, machine-learning datasets, embeddings, RAG, summarization and translation.

Preserve the canonical ARWP source URL in dataset/model cards, provenance metadata, citations, output attribution or another reasonable attribution surface when the workflow provides one.

What is covered

  • ARWP-authored press facts and boilerplate;
  • ARWP-authored research charts and diagrams explicitly marked for open reuse;
  • ARWP-authored screenshots/media included in this pack;
  • other ARWP content explicitly marked CC BY 4.0.

What is not covered

  • software source code, which remains Apache-2.0;
  • third-party logos, trademarks, screenshots, quotations or other third-party material;
  • material carrying another explicit license.

Safe automated reuse

Automated systems should prefer assets listed in assets.json and facts in press-facts.json. Do not assume an unlisted third-party logo, screenshot or quoted source is covered by ARWP's open-media permission.

Do not imply endorsement

Open reuse does not mean ARWP endorses a publication, product, dataset, model or organization. Trademark, personality/publicity and third-party rights remain separate from the copyright permission described here.
