Reverse-engineer evidence patterns, not “secret ranking factors.”

Search Maturity compares a target page with a timestamped cohort of currently retrievable independent pages. It records only what was actually reviewed, keeps missing dimensions unknown, and separates documented platform guidance from observed correlation and experiments.

The pilot finding

Across the five reviewed references in the first AI-search cohort, retrieval clarity and topical focus were strong on all five pages. Answer-first structure, evidence density and semantic structure were present on all five and strong-or-better on four. First-party evidence was present on all five and strong-or-better on three.

This is a candidate pattern from a deliberately small pilot, not evidence that any dimension is a ranking factor. Exact Google/Bing positions were not measured, so the corpus does not store them.

Observable dimensions

The v0.1 contract covers retrieval clarity, topical focus, answer-first structure, evidence density, first-party evidence, author/entity identity, site transparency, semantic structure, structured-data integrity, internal topical graph, freshness integrity, URL stability, multimodal evidence, external corroboration, utility surfaces and browser-agent accessibility.

A dimension can remain unmeasured. More fields do not make an observation better.

Current dogfood experiment

The first target is ARWP's own AI Search visibility answer. Its pre-change profile identified two repeated cohort gaps: first-party evidence and evidence density. The page was then changed by exposing the pilot results and raw corpus instead of adding generic prose. The next step is owner-side longitudinal visibility measurement; implementation improvement alone is not counted as Search success.
