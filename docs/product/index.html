The product loop

Goose should feel simple even though the evidence machinery underneath it is deep.

Tell Goose the goal → Inspect → Decide → Change → Prove → Watch / revise.

  • Inspect: observe the real site, intent ownership, entities/data, crawl/index state, repository ownership and applicable machine surfaces.
  • Decide: return the top one to three moves that actually apply, with source/evidence and explicit unknowns.
  • Change: prepare deterministic, reviewable mutations where source ownership and policy boundaries are known.
  • Prove: distinguish implementation verification, deployment parity and later Search/AI outcomes.
  • Watch: re-review recommendations when upstream guidance, site state or observed outcomes change.

Why no Goose score

Search and AI providers expose different evidence. Goose keeps stages separate instead of flattening them into a universal 0–100 number.

access → index/eligibility → exposure → citation/reference → brand mention → visit → useful action

Google generative-AI impressions are not Bing citations. Crawler access is not a citation result. Citation is not traffic or conversion. Missing evidence remains unknown.

Current priority: proof before more product

The immediate product milestone is real site evidence, not another module. Goose is prioritizing owned-site experiments with exact deployment parity, provider-native evidence and explicit keep/revise/continue/revert/retire decisions. Neutral and negative outcomes remain visible.

Product line · Roadmap

Search / GEO boundary

Goose does not claim that special AI markup, schema volume, llms.txt, policy-page count or any one technical artifact guarantees ranking or citation. Current Google guidance says foundational SEO remains applicable to AI Overviews and AI Mode and that no additional special AI optimization is required for eligibility.

The practical focus is useful non-commodity content, intent/page ownership, crawl/index eligibility, truthful identity/data, evidence/provenance and real provider-native outcome measurement after deployment.
