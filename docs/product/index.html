How it works

Choose a real problem on your site. Check what is happening today, make a sensible change and see what happened afterwards.

Tell Goose the goal → Inspect → Decide → Change → Prove → Watch / revise.

  • Inspect: check the page people should find, whether it loads and what might be getting in their way.
  • Decide: choose one to three relevant fixes and see why they may help. Unknowns stay visible.
  • Change: prepare a specific, reviewable edit when the right source file and permissions are known.
  • Check: confirm the change is actually on the live page. Later, look for evidence of a real outcome.
  • Review: revisit the advice when search guidance changes or the results do not match expectations.

Why we don’t give you one magic score

A page can pass every technical check and still get few relevant visits. We show the checks and observations separately so you know what each number means.

access → index/eligibility → exposure → citation/reference → brand mention → visit → useful action

Google generative-AI impressions are not Bing citations. Crawler access is not a citation result. Citation is not traffic or conversion. Missing evidence remains unknown.

What we’re working to prove

Before adding more features, we want to know which improvements genuinely help. That means checking the actual published pages, comparing the available search results and recording changes that did nothing as well as those that helped.

Product line · Roadmap

Search / GEO boundary

Goose does not claim that special AI markup, schema volume, llms.txt, policy-page count or any one technical artifact guarantees ranking or citation. Current Google guidance says foundational SEO remains applicable to AI Overviews and AI Mode and that no additional special AI optimization is required for eligibility.

The practical focus is useful non-commodity content, intent/page ownership, crawl/index eligibility, truthful identity/data, evidence/provenance and real provider-native outcome measurement after deployment.
