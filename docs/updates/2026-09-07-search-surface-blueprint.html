Search Surface Blueprint: deeper current-search architecture

ARWP now separates “what current Search systems actually document” from “which useful site surfaces make sense for this kind of product,” and keeps technical edge cases evidence-dependent instead of universal.

What changed

The Blueprint now contains 69 source-backed or explicitly conditional checks across site identity, search presentation, crawl/indexing, freshness, visual discovery, page archetypes, AI citation, mobile/rendering, internationalization, image/video discovery and anti-abuse policy guardrails.

It also models 18 page surfaces: home, product, services, docs, updates, changelog, roadmap, case studies, evidence, research, datasets, profile, about, events, comparisons, trust, media and topic hubs.

Why page types are conditional

A software product often benefits from Product, Docs, Changelog and Maintainer pages. A news publisher has a different architecture. A portfolio does not become better by manufacturing a news sitemap or a fake roadmap. The analyzer therefore classifies the site first and emits required, recommended and opportunity surfaces for that archetype.

44 core Search + surface checks

  • host-level site name and favicon boundaries;
  • Googlebot 2 MB fetch-limit risk for critical HTML bytes;
  • preferred page images and large-image preview eligibility;
  • publication dates and article authorship;
  • sitemap/canonical and internal-link coverage;
  • software version and release-note linkage;
  • roadmap/changelog collection semantics without rich-result claims;
  • Bing AI citation clarity, evidence and cross-format consistency.

25 deeper technical and policy checks

  • mobile-first content and metadata parity;
  • JavaScript lazy loading, infinite scroll and crawlable pagination;
  • noindex/robots interaction and state-changing URLs;
  • localized variants, hreflang and conditional x-default;
  • HTML image discovery, alt text, stable image URLs and image sitemaps;
  • dedicated video watch pages, VideoObject, video sitemaps and no user-action dependency;
  • structured-data parity with visible facts;
  • scaled-content, doorway, third-party content and current site-reputation-abuse guardrails;
  • crawl-budget advice only for sites where scale makes it relevant.

Evidence-dependent automation

All checks are in the registry, but ARWP does not auto-fire categories it cannot safely infer. Public crawl evidence can resolve canonical/title/page size/article/image/sitemap issues. Mobile parity, authenticated platform state, hreflang completeness, policy abuse and similar concerns remain conditional, owner-data or manual-review checks until supporting evidence exists.

How ARWP uses it

arwp-surfaces can run independently, while arwp-improve merges its applicable findings with Growth, Entity Graph and page-link evidence so the site receives a small prioritized backlog rather than a giant checklist.

Boundaries

This update does not claim that Roadmap, Changelog, FAQ, NewsArticle or any Schema.org type is a ranking factor. Structured data must match visible facts, optional surfaces should be omitted when thin or artificial, and passing a checklist does not prove indexing, ranking, AI citation or recommendation inclusion.
